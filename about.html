About

As a kid, I wanted to be a hockey player. That didn’t happen, so I became a designer instead.

I’ve always been pulled toward beauty, the kind of order in things that just feels right. That pull took me from industrial design to graphic design before landing on product design, where it finally clicked, the same eye for beauty, but now with the logic and structure to make it actually work.

Six years in, across B2B SaaS and consumer mobile, and I still love it. Right now I’m deep in folding AI into my process, not to skip the thinking, just to move faster through it.

What I’ve learned along the way: most things are possible if you keep going, stay consistent, and always know your next step.
